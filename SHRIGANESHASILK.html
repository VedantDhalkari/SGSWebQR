About Shri Ganesha Silk

Shri Ganesha Silk is a saree and traditional-wear shop located at Ganesh Peth, near Ganesh Temple, in Basmath, Maharashtra. We welcome you to visit our store and see our collection of sarees and traditional wear in person.
